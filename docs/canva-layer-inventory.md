## Grouping exceptions

The opening couple portrait with its ribbon frame, each profile portrait with its immediately attached blue flower frame, and the closing portrait with its gold hexagonal frame are grouped. Each pair behaves as one masked/framed photograph, and Canva only exposed protected previews for the uploaded portraits. All surrounding text and unrelated ornaments remain separate. Arabic calligraphy remains an isolated image because it is authored artwork, not ordinary body text.
